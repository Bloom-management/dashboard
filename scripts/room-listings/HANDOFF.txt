Bloom grouped room visits — local handoff, 2026-11-08

Implemented
The existing job is the claimable house visit. Individual room turnovers retain
separate stable IDs linked to the existing calendar event or manual booking.
A house/day unique index and house transaction lock prevent separate bedroom
jobs and concurrent duplicate visits. Existing entire-home paths delegate to
their previous functions; private-team assignment, compensation and decline
paths remain available.

Accepted rooms determine the title, bedroom requirements, shared requirements,
cleaner pool and host charge snapshots. Shared bathroom, kitchen and living-room
spaces occur once. Both assigned cleaners share space completion and photos;
either can submit completion. Backup stock and maintenance reports have their
own author/time/revision history and never mark a space cleaned. Shared reports
and recipient notices are deduplicated.

An added room after claiming creates a versioned proposal. Original accepted
requirements and compensation stay unchanged until every active assignment
accepts. Decline leaves original assignments/pay intact and flags coverage for
owner/admin review. Assignment, date, membership, pricing and visit-version
changes invalidate stale responses. Additions after starting require owner/admin
coordination. Claims and proposal mutations are receipt-protected on retry.

Extensions keep the turnover identity. Unclaimed turns can move to the correct
local date and join another eligible visit. Changes to accepted/started work
hold it for review, retaining its accepted date, assignments and payout; they do
not move it or publish another solo cleaning. Pending cancellations do not
reprice accepted work. Completed jobs/payments stay frozen; late rooms become
coverage exceptions. Owner/admin cancellation is explicit and recorded.

Physical space setup uses the canonical house spaces, rather than making four
copies of the shared spaces. Supplies/maintenance are available in Properties
and the cleaner visit through Room/Shared controls. Owners/admins can create,
rename, remove and report shared or room-only items. Prices are configurable
through Visit pricing; updates are audited, never rewrite accepted snapshots,
and invalidate pending offers. Legacy per-listing/job pricing overrides are
blocked for room visits so they cannot bypass consent.

Active grouped-visit photos remain limited to assigned cleaners and authorized
owners/admins. Completed summaries and photos are also read-only for other eligible
cleaners, following entire-home city/private-team eligibility. Completed cleaner
views do not expose current access codes. Pending bedroom codes remain hidden.
